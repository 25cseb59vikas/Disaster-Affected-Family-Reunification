End-to-End Technical Pipeline & Field Protocol

Preserved Architecture

REUNITE AI adheres strictly to the offline-first humanitarian workflow designed for austere disaster zones where telecom infrastructure is destroyed.

1

PWA & Offline Voice AI

Field responders capture spoken or written dispatches. Offline AI parses names, ages, kinship relations, and physical clues locally.

2

Local IndexedDB Vault

Information is normalized and committed instantly to browser IndexedDB storage without requiring network connectivity.

3

Simulated SATCOM Mesh

When satellite window opens, delta records are serialized and broadcast to remote disaster relief centers via simulated SATCOM.

4

Multi-Factor Matching

The matching engine ranks cross-camp candidates using explainable weights: 65% Name, 20% Relative Kinship, 15% Age Delta.

5

Human Verification

Authorized relief staff verify identity dossiers side-by-side to prevent false positives and protect vulnerable populations.

6

Family Notification

Once verified by authority, official emergency SMS, camp P.A. broadcasts, and transport passes are dispatched to reunite the family.
